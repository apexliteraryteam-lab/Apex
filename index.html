Amazon rewards concentrated sales bursts. Without a structured launch campaign, books disappear within days.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the team communicate during campaigns?

All communications are conducted exclusively via email at apexliteraryteam@gmail.com. Every aspect is permanently documented and fully accountable.

How do I get a quote for my campaign?

Email your book details to apexliteraryteam@gmail.com or complete the contact form. We respond with a customized proposal within 24 to 48 hours.

Do you work with debut authors?

Yes. Many successful campaigns have been for debut authors with zero audience. We build systems driving targeted traffic directly to your book.

Can you revitalize older books?

Yes. We audit titles, rewrite descriptions, update keywords, restructure categories, and launch targeted promotions to re-engage algorithms.

Do you produce book trailers?

Yes. Our creative team produces cinematic trailers, short-form social reels, ad-ready video creatives, and author brand videos. See our Showcase section.

Do you guarantee sales numbers?

No ethical agency guarantees exact sales. We guarantee every deliverable will be executed to professional standards with complete documentation.

Will I approve marketing materials?

Yes. You retain full creative control. All assets are submitted for your written approval before deployment. Nothing published without your consent.
